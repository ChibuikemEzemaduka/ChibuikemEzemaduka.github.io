How I think

Fundamentals first.
Then make it survive reality.

The through-line in my work is not a single tool. It is a method: understand the system, formalize the decision, build the evidence, test the failure modes, and make the result usable.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Start with how the physical, human, or operational system actually behaves.

  2. 02

    Formalize

    Turn ambiguity into a measurable objective, constraints, and honest assumptions.

  3. 03

    Build

    Choose models and infrastructure that fit the problem and the data.

  4. 04

    Break it

    Probe privacy, security, edge cases, and what happens beyond the clean benchmark.

  5. 05

    Operationalize

    Translate the result into a decision, workflow, deployment, or next experiment.
